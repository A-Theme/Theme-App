Aramaki's Theme Editor

Build a theme.json for CyberFoil — or convert one of your RomM themes into one

Theme details

Colours

A role you leave unset is not a colour you forgot: CyberFoil keeps its own value, and highlight and scrollbar follow accent. The grey placeholder is what each one resolves to right now.

Background

none
0.00
CyberFoil has no dim setting, so the editor darkens the image itself when it exports: c × (1 − dim), the same multiply the RomM client applies. Author at 1280×720 — the image is drawn at its own size, not scaled.

Banner

none
system font
CyberFoil keeps its own UI font, so the banner is the one place a theme's typeface can still appear. The font is used to draw it here and does not ship.
Drawn at 0,−8 and fitted to 480×93 whatever its size, so a banner of any other shape is stretched.

Icons & mascot

Each one replaces the built-in image at that path. Transparent PNGs, square for the icons. Change both checkbox images or neither — a half-changed pair reads as two different controls.

    theme.json

    Installing: the pack unzips to a folder — drop the whole folder into sdmc:/switch/CyberFoil/theme/ on your SD card, then pick it in Settings → System → Theme. It applies at once, no restart. A theme with no images can also go in as a single .json file straight inside theme/.
    Everything here runs in your browser. No file you choose is uploaded anywhere. CyberFoil's own artwork in the preview is loaded from luketanti/CyberFoil and is not part of this editor.

    Convert a RomM theme

    Published themes from A-Theme/RomM-Themes. Opening one maps its 19 roles onto CyberFoil's 11, darkens its background by its own dim, and draws a banner in its font.
