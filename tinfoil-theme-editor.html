ℹ️ How color + transparency (alpha) worksclick to expand

Tinfoil colors are hex codes made of up to 4 pairs of characters: Red, Green, Blue, and an optional 4th pair for Alpha (transparency). Alpha isn't part of the color itself — it controls how see-through that color is when drawn on screen.

d9a9937a
RedGreenBlueAlpha

The alpha pair ranges from 00 (completely invisible) to ff (completely solid) — everything in between is partial transparency:

00 — fully invisible 80 — 50% see-through ff — fully solid

Any color field with an alpha pair gets its own transparency slider right below its color picker in the form — drag it to adjust see-through-ness without touching the hex text, and watch the checkered swatch next to it update live so you can see exactly what you're getting.
